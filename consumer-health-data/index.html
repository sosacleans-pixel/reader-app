Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

22-Day 7-Minute Workout app (app.coachsalimheyaime.com)
Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This is our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. It applies to residents of Washington, Nevada, Connecticut and other states with similar consumer health data laws, and to anyone whose consumer health data is collected in Washington or Nevada (Washington's My Health My Data Act, RCW 19.373, Nevada's NRS 603A.400 to 603A.550, and the consumer health data provisions of the Connecticut Data Privacy Act). It covers the 22-Day 7-Minute Workout app at app.coachsalimheyaime.com (the "app"), the companion app to the book A Challenge to Move, and your purchase of the app on achallengetomove.com. The app is provided by Coach Salim Heyaime LLC, a Utah limited liability company in Herriman, Utah ("we", "us"). Our general Privacy Policy covers everything else.

Consumer health data we collect, and how we use it

  • Daily check-ins (only with your consent). Your energy before and after a workout (1 to 5), your mood (1 to 5), and the reflections you write (up to 2,000 characters). We use them only to show them back to you in the app.
  • Workout log. For each day you complete: the calendar date, which of the 22 days it was, and the time you marked it complete. We use it to run your 22-day calendar, your 30-day window and your progress.
  • Streaks. Your current and best streak, total workouts, last workout date and time zone. We use them to show your streak and to pick the right reminder message.
  • Reminder settings and push subscriptions. Your workout time, time zone, whether push or email reminders are on, reminder delivery records, and, if you turn on push, your browser's push address, its encryption keys and a shortened browser user-agent string. We use them only to send the reminders you asked for.
  • Account and purchase information. Your email address and account ID, sign-in times and the technical details of sign-in requests (such as IP address and browser type), and, if you buy the app, your name, email address and purchase details. Because the app is a fitness program, having an account or buying it could suggest you are trying to improve your health, so we treat this as consumer health data too. We use it to sign you in, deliver your access, send receipts and service messages, handle refunds and support, keep accounts secure, and keep the records tax law requires.
  • Messages you send us. Whatever you choose to write when you email us. We use it only to answer you.

We do not collect precise location, and we do not use geofencing. We do not collect data from wearables, phones or health apps. Please don't put medical details (diagnoses, medications, test results, health care details) or other people's information in your reflections.

Your consent

We collect your energy, mood and reflections only after you give consent with a separate, clearly worded checkbox that is never ticked for you, shown before you save your first day. We record the date and time you agree. The rest of the data above is what we need to provide the app you asked for. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app with the Withdraw consent option or by emailing us, and we will stop collecting check-ins right away. We will ask for your separate consent before we ever share consumer health data with anyone other than the service providers listed below, and we will never sell it.

If you saved check-ins before we added this consent step, the app asks you the next time you open it, with the same two boxes. If you leave the check-in box unticked, or don't answer within 30 days of the notice we email you, we delete the energy, mood and reflections you saved before. Your kept days stay. We never assume consent you didn't give.

Where the data comes from

  • From you, when you sign in, use the app, turn on reminders, or email us.
  • From your browser and device: your time zone, push subscription details, browser type and IP address.
  • From Stripe, which confirms your payment and sends us your name, email address and purchase details.

Who we share it with

We share consumer health data only with the service providers (processors) below, and only so they can provide their service to us:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and reminder scheduling): all of the app data listed above.
  • Your browser's push service (for example Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on your browser), only if you turn on push reminders: your push address and encrypted reminder messages, which may mention your streak.
  • [Resend — CONFIRM] (email reminders and account notices): your email address, reminder messages if you turn on email reminders (they may mention your streak), and account notices such as a policy update or an inactive-account warning. Check-ins and reflections are never put in these emails.

The categories of consumer health data we share are the ones listed for each provider above. We have no affiliates that receive consumer health data. We may disclose information if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid court order.

Never sold, never used for advertising

We never sell your personal information or consumer health data, and we never use it, or share it, for advertising. The app screens after you sign in have no analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. Our public welcome screen, before you sign in, uses Google Analytics only if you choose "Accept analytics" (and never when your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal). It never receives your consumer health data, as our Privacy Policy explains.

Your rights and how to use them

You can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell consumer health data about you;
  • give you access to that data, together with a list of every third party and affiliate we have shared it with or sold it to (and their contact information, if you ask);
  • withdraw your consent, and stop collecting or sharing your consumer health data;
  • delete it. We will delete it from our systems and tell every service provider we shared it with to delete it too. Data in rolling backups is deleted when those backups roll over [CONFIRM: e.g., within 7 days] and will not be restored. We keep only records the law requires us to keep, such as tax and payment records;
  • correct your email address or other account details.

In the app you can delete your account and all of your data yourself with the Delete my account and data button (deletion from our database is immediate), withdraw consent for check-ins, and download a copy of your data as a JSON file. By email, write to coachsalimheyaime@gmail.com with "Consumer health data request" in the subject line, from (or including) the email address you sign in with. To protect your data, we will confirm the request comes from you, for example by asking you to reply from that address or sign in to the app. We will respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we will tell you within those 45 days and may take up to 45 more days. Requests are free (in Washington, up to twice in any 12-month period), and we won't treat you differently for making one. An authorized agent may make a request on your behalf if you give them written permission and we can confirm your identity.

Appeals. If we decline your request, in whole or in part, you can appeal by replying to our answer with "Appeal" in the subject line. We will answer your appeal in writing within 45 days and explain our decision. If we deny your appeal, you can contact the Washington Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint or the Nevada Attorney General at ag.nv.gov.

How we protect it

Consumer health data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Database rules let each signed-in person read and change only their own records. Access inside our business is limited to the people who need it to run, support or secure the app.
