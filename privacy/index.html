4. Consumer health data

Your energy and mood ratings and your reflections are consumer health data under Washington and Nevada law, because they describe your physical or mental state. Because this is a fitness app, we also treat the rest of your app data (your workout log, streaks, reminder settings, and the fact that you have an account) as consumer health data, and we protect it the same way. See the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for the full details and your rights.

5. Your consent before your first log

Before you save your first day, the app shows a separate consent box that is never ticked for you. We record the date and time you agree. It explains what the optional check-in collects (energy, mood and reflections), why, where it is stored, who processes it for us, and how to withdraw. If you tick it, the app saves your check-ins with your account. If you don't, you can still do the workouts and keep your days on the calendar; the energy, mood and reflection fields stay off.

If you saved check-ins before we added this consent step, the app asks you the next time you open it, with the same two boxes. If you leave the check-in box unticked, or don't answer within 30 days of the notice we email you, we delete the energy, mood and reflections you saved before. Your kept days stay. We never assume consent you didn't give.

You can withdraw your consent at any time in the app with the Withdraw consent option or by emailing us. When you withdraw, we stop collecting check-ins right away, and the app gives you the choice to delete the check-ins you already saved. Withdrawing doesn't affect anything we did with your consent before you withdrew.

8. Analytics and tracking

Our public pages, before you sign in (the app's welcome and sign-in screen), use Google Analytics. It collects the pages viewed, your approximate location (based on your IP address), your device and browser type, the referral source (the site or link that brought you to us), and that a sign-in link was requested. It never receives your name or email address. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes this data under Google's own privacy policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

The app screens after you sign in don't use Google Analytics or any other analytics service, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies, and the app does not track you across other websites or apps. Our website achallengetomove.com uses Google Analytics as described in its own privacy policy.

9. Push notifications and email reminders

Reminders are off until you turn them on. Push reminders also need your browser's permission, which your browser asks for separately. Reminders come at most once a day around the time you picked. If you stop working out, "welcome back" reminders taper off and then stop on their own. Notifications can appear on your lock screen, so other people with access to your device may see them (for example, your streak count). To stop them, turn reminders off in the app (the ⏰ button), or turn off notifications for this site in your browser or phone settings. Email reminders include a way to turn them off. Reminders are service messages about the app you asked for, not marketing.

10. How long we keep your information

  • Account and app data: while your account is open, until you delete it or ask us to.
  • Starting over: if a 30-day window ends before you complete the 22 days, or if you choose "Start this commitment over", the app deletes that round's workout log and check-ins from our servers.
  • Inactive accounts: if 24 months pass since your last sign-in or your last logged day, whichever is later, we delete your account and all of its data (your workout log, check-ins and reflections, streaks, reminder settings, push subscriptions, consent records and profile). We email you at least 30 days before, and signing in before the date in that email keeps your account.
  • Push subscriptions: until you turn push off, sign out of push, or the push service tells us the subscription no longer works.
  • Backups: our database provider keeps backups on a rolling schedule [CONFIRM: e.g., 7 days on the current Supabase plan]. Deleted data drops out of backups when they roll over, and we won't restore it.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires (generally up to 7 years). These don't include your workout data.

11. Deleting your account, your logs, and getting a copy

In the app: the Delete my account and data button permanently deletes your account (including your sign-in email), workout log, check-ins, streaks, reminder settings and push subscriptions from our servers right away, and clears the copy on that device. "Start this commitment over" (and the automatic reset when a 30-day window ends early) deletes only that round's workout log and check-ins; your account, streaks and reminder settings stay until you delete your account.

By email: email coachsalimheyaime@gmail.com from the email you sign in with and ask us to delete your account or your logs. We will confirm the request comes from you and complete it within 45 days (we'll usually be much faster). We will also tell our service providers to delete it.

A copy of your data: the app's Download my data option gives you a JSON file with your account email, workout log, check-ins, streaks and reminder settings. You can also ask us by email, and we will send it within 45 days.

Deleting your account does not refund your purchase (see the Terms of Use) and does not delete purchase records we must keep by law.

12. Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is encrypted at rest by our database provider. Database rules allow each signed-in person to read and change only their own records. Sign-in uses one-time email links, so there's no password to steal, but anyone who can open your email can sign in to your account, so keep your email secure. Only Coach Salim Heyaime LLC and the people who build and support the app for us can access the database, and only when needed to run, support or secure the app. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.
